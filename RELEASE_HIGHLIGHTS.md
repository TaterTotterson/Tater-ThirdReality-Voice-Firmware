- Releases ThirdReality S420 firmware `0.2.23` with complete Sendspin compatibility
  for Music Assistant 2.10.x and Tater's synchronized reply playback.
- Makes S420 players appear correctly in Music Assistant by using its supported
  pairing, player-control, and delay formats over the encrypted Sendspin session.
- Restores Tater reply playback through S420 stereo pairs while retaining FLAC and
  PCM music playback, channel selection, synchronized timing, and volume control.
- Preserves microWakeWord, openWakeWord, Dual Wake Word, AEC, passive BLE presence,
  timer controls, and signed OTA updates from the previous release.
